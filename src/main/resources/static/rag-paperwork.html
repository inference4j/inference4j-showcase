The front desk scanned Alice's pickup checklist, her receipt and the damage report from her e-bike rental. She asks the support chat what is on them. The scans are read with OCR, personal data is redacted, and a guardrail screens every message before it reaches the model. Every model runs in this JVM, behind LangChain4j interfaces.

  1. Inference4jOcrDocumentParser reads each scan with PP-OCRv6, one document per form.
  2. Inference4jPiiRedactor replaces the customer's name, phone, email and ID number with placeholders before anything is stored. The shop's own reference numbers are kept.
  3. Inference4jEmbeddingModel embeds the forms into an in-memory LangChain4j store.
  4. An AiServices assistant retrieves the two closest forms, runs Inference4jPromptInjectionGuardrail on the message, and Phi-4-mini answers from the forms. A blocked message never reaches the model.

First use downloads about 6 GB of models (Phi-4-mini is 4.6 GB). Every model is limited to 4 CPU threads. Each question is answered on its own, without chat history. OCR reads printed text; it does not read handwriting.
