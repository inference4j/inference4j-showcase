The recordings are transcribed, personal data is redacted, and the answer points to the moment in the call where it was said. Every model runs in this JVM, behind LangChain4j interfaces.

  1. Inference4jAudioDocumentParser transcribes each recording with Moonshine, cut at pauses by Silero VAD; mergeSegments(300) joins neighbouring pieces into excerpts that carry their start time.
  2. Inference4jPiiRedactor replaces names and phone numbers with placeholders before anything is stored.
  3. Inference4jEmbeddingModel embeds the excerpts into an in-memory LangChain4j store.
  4. An AiServices assistant retrieves the three closest excerpts and Phi-4-mini, behind Inference4jStreamingChatModel, answers from them. The sources come from the retrieved excerpts, so each answer can play the moment it is based on.

First use downloads about 6 GB of models (Phi-4-mini is 4.6 GB). Every model is limited to 4 CPU threads. Each question is answered on its own, without chat history.
